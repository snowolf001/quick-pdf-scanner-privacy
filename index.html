Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Quick PDF Scanner respects your privacy. This application does not collect, store, or share your personal information.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Quick PDF Scanner app on all supported platforms, including Android and iOS.

Camera Access

The app uses your device camera solely for scanning documents. Images are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Photos and Files

The app may access photos or files that you choose to import. Documents and generated PDF files are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly share them using your device's sharing features.

Data Collection

Quick PDF Scanner does not collect, store, or transmit your personal or sensitive information.

Data Sharing

The app does not share your data with third parties. Any document sharing is initiated entirely by you through your device's built-in sharing options.

Internet Connection

Quick PDF Scanner performs document scanning and PDF generation locally on your device. An internet connection is not required for normal scanning and PDF creation.
